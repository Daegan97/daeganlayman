A Policy Critique of Nevada Administrative Code 228Start here

Nevada requires domestic-violence offenders to complete treatment but lets minimally trained staff deliver it. This critique argues for higher qualifications, backs the case with recidivism research, and works through funding, staffing and stakeholders.

Assessing and Improving Police Morale and Community Safety in the ‘Post-Ferguson’ Era

Did public criticism after Ferguson drive down police morale? This paper weighs the evidence, finds a weak but real link, and lays out a plan for regulation, accountability and stakeholder buy-in, with measures to tell whether it worked.

Graduate paper, M.S. Criminal Justice (CSU Global), 2021. 11 min read.

Memorandum on Policies Concerning Family Factors and Juvenile Offending

A policy memo arguing, from longitudinal research and an evaluated family-violence program, that the most effective place to reduce juvenile offending is the family, and recommending court-mandated family support programs.

Investigating a Potential Relationship between Race and Frequency of Police Contact

A one-way ANOVA in SPSS on the 2008 Police-Public Contact Survey (Bureau of Justice Statistics, via ICPSR; 9,522 respondents) testing whether how often people have face-to-face contact with police differs by race. It found no significant difference, and the discussion is careful about what that does and doesn’t show.

Graduate paper, Analytical Methods, M.S. Criminal Justice (CSU Global), 2022. 9 min read.
